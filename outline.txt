Notes and cautions


- Not closed loop: nitric tail‑gas is treated and vented; building ventilation and condenser vents exist.

- O2 to WWTP must remain supplemental; throttle with flow control so you don’t oversupply.

- Keep hypochlorite generation tiny—only for your water/WWTP polishing doses.

- The above procurement costs are “base +35% adders”; applying an extra plant‑level 20% volatility reserve will push totals higher (intentionally pessimistic).

- The OPEX allocations intentionally embed a high maintenance burden (4% of procurement/year) and full labor at 20 FTE.
